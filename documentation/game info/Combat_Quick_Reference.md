## Telegraph Color Guide
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Low threat (dodge easily)
- 🟠 **Orange** = Medium threat (dodge recommended)
- 🔴 **Dark Red** = Extreme threat (dodge critical!)

## Movement Patterns
### Melee Enemies
- Chase player aggressively
- Circle when in range
- Stop to attack

### Ranged Enemies
- Maintain optimal distance
- Backpedal when too close
- Strafe to avoid attacks

## Damage Scaling
All damage scales with:
1. Wave number (increases each wave)
2. Enemy buffs/debuffs
3. Berserker mode (<30% health = +50% damage)

## Combat Tips
- **Goblins**: Fast but weak - burst them down
- **Orcs**: Time dodges for their heavy swings
- **Skeleton/Wizard**: Close distance to disrupt
- **Golems**: Massive telegraphs - easy to dodge
- **Elementals**: Unpredictable - stay mobile

## Visual Cues
- **Yellow pulse** = Attack incoming
- **Red flash** = Enemy attacking NOW
- **Orange particles** = Hit confirmed
- **Scale changes** = Attack animation
